# Passage 002: GPT model ki kahani

Read exactly as written. About 60 seconds.

GPT ek aisa program hai jo agla shabd guess karta hai. Bas itna. Tum likho "aaj mausam", woh bolega "accha hai". Lekin yeh guess itni baar aur itne data par sikha hai ki poori baat samajh leta hai. Kahani 2017 mein shuru hui, jab Google ke kuch researchers ne ek paper likha, naam tha "Attention is all you need". Usmein unhone Transformer banaya, ek aisa design jo poore sentence ko ek saath dekhta hai, ek ek shabd nahi. Agle saal OpenAI ne is design par pehla GPT banaya, matlab Generative Pre-trained Transformer. Pehle usko internet ka dher saara text padhaya, phir chhote kaam sikhaye. 2020 mein GPT-3 aaya, ek sau pachhattar billion parameters, aur log hairan reh gaye ki yeh kavita bhi likh leta hai aur code bhi. Phir November 2022 mein ChatGPT aaya, jisse aam aadmi baat kar sakta tha, aur paanch din mein ek million log jud gaye. Kyun bana? Kyunki computer se baat karne ka sapna purana tha, aur pehli baar data, chips aur yeh design teeno ek saath mil gaye.
