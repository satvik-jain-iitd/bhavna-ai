# Passage 006: AlphaFold, biology aur AI ka mel

Read exactly as written. About 60 seconds.

Har zinda cheez protein se bani hai, aur protein ka kaam uske aakaar se tay hota hai. Pachaas saal tak biology ka sabse bada sawaal yahi tha: agar mujhe protein ke amino acid ki chain pata hai, to woh teen dimension mein kaise mudegi? Ek protein ka aakaar lab mein nikalne mein saalon lagte the aur karodon rupaye. 2020 mein London ki company DeepMind ne, Demis Hassabis aur John Jumper ki team ke saath, AlphaFold 2 banaya. Yeh AI model chain dekh kar aakaar bata deta tha, utni sateekta se jitni lab mein milti thi. Agle saal unhone do crore se zyada proteins ke aakaar muft mein sabko de diye, yaani lagbhag har protein jo vigyan ko pata hai. Isse dawa banane waale, malaria aur cancer par kaam karne waale, sab ka kaam saalon se ghat kar hafton mein aa gaya. 2024 mein Hassabis aur Jumper ko Chemistry ka Nobel Prize mila. Yeh pehli baar tha jab AI ne biology ka ek purana taala khola, aur dikhaya ki computer sirf baat nahi karta, khoj bhi kar sakta hai.
