GPT jaise models System Two hain, woh har baat par lamba sochte hain aur phir likhte hain. Lekin software ke andar zyadatar faisle chhote hote hain. Yeh email urgent hai ya nahi, yeh ticket kis team ko jaaye, is review mein gussa hai ya khushi. Inke liye lamba likhna bekaar hai. Isi soch se TypeSafe AI ne September 2026 mein Jev nikala, aur isko System One model ka naam diya. Jev prose nahi likhta. Jawab sattar se paanch sau millisecond mein aata hai. Naam Jevons se aaya, us economist se jisne kaha tha ki cheez sasti ho to uska istemaal badh jaata hai. Toh GPT baat karne ke liye hai, aur Jev faisle lene ke liye, tez aur typed.
