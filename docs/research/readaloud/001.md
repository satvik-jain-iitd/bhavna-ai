# Passage 001: Compound interest (chakravriddhi byaaj)

Read exactly as written. About 45 seconds.

Chakravriddhi byaaj ka matlab hai byaaj par byaaj. Maan lo tumne bank mein das hazaar rupaye rakhe aur bank har saal das pratishat deta hai. Pehle saal ke baad tumhare paas gyaarah hazaar honge. Doosre saal bank sirf das hazaar par nahi, poore gyaarah hazaar par byaaj dega, to tumhe gyaarah sau milenge, aur total baarah hazaar ek sau ho jayega. Yahi fark hai simple interest aur compound interest mein. Simple mein har saal ek hazaar milta, compound mein har saal thoda zyada. Dus saal mein das hazaar lagbhag pachchees hazaar ban jaate hain, bina kuch kiye. Isliye log kehte hain ki jaldi shuru karo, kyunki samay hi asli taakat hai.
