# Passage 003: JEPA, aur GPT se fark

Read exactly as written. About 60 seconds.

Yann LeCun, jo Meta ke chief AI scientist hain, ek baat baar baar kehte hain: ek billi bhi duniya ko GPT se behtar samajhti hai. Unka kehna hai ki agla shabd guess karna samajh nahi hai, woh sirf ratta hai. Isliye 2022 mein unhone ek paper likha, "A Path Towards Autonomous Machine Intelligence", aur usmein JEPA ka idea rakha. JEPA ka matlab Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture. Fark simple hai. GPT har chhoti detail predict karta hai, har shabd, har pixel. JEPA detail nahi, matlab predict karta hai. Jaise tum ek photo ka aadha hissa dekh kar bata dete ho ki baaki hisse mein kya hoga, bina har pixel banaye. 2023 mein Meta ne I-JEPA nikala, jo photo par kaam karta tha. 2024 mein V-JEPA aaya, jo video dekh kar samajhta hai ki aage kya hone wala hai. Aur 2025 mein V-JEPA 2 aaya, jisse robot ne bina sikhaye naye kaam kiye. Toh GPT bolne mein ustaad hai, aur JEPA dekhne aur samajhne ki taraf ek koshish hai. Dono ka raasta alag hai, manzil ek.
