# ADR-015: A cascade picks the model per chunk from slack: Prime early, Apex later, Swift when time is short.

Status: proposed, 2026-10-27. Refines ADR-013. Becomes accepted only if the A/B in Phase 6 passes.

Context: every chunk goes through Apex (`PROFILE="lean"`). In 592 timed dictations the release-to-text p90 is 9 to 12.5 s. In the slow ones the final chunk has p50 5.8 s and the backlog wait has p90 10.2 s. Early chunks have time to spare, because he is still talking. Only the final chunk is on the critical path.
Decision: `PROFILE="cascade"` uses three models. Plain code picks one per chunk, no LLM.
- While he talks: `pick(len, backlog, e)` returns Prime if `backlog + len x RTF_prime <= expected_left(e)`, else Apex if that fits, else Swift. `expected_left(e)` is the median talk time still to come, from his own `stats.jsonl` (with a fixed fallback under 30 rows).
- At release: `pick_last(len)` returns Apex if `len x RTF_apex <= 0.7 s`, else Swift.
- Under `RAM_FLOOR_MB` free, Prime is never picked.
- A chunk done by Swift is queued for Prime again only while he talks and slack remains. Never after release. **Not wired yet:** `redo_with_prime` is a tested pure function, but `main()` does not call it. Wire it only if the replay bench shows Swift-done chunks are common.
- Prime loads on the first dictation longer than `PRIME_MIN_TALK_S` and unloads after `PRIME_IDLE_S` idle.
Models run only after the numbers: if Prime is not at least 2 WER points better than Apex, or Prime and Apex do not fit in RAM together, the cascade is Apex plus Swift (Phase 2 decision rule).
Trade-off: three model folders on disk (Prime is 1.5 GB), a load cost for Prime, and a scheduler to keep honest. A wrong RTF makes wrong picks, so RTF is measured on the M1 (T2.4), logged per chunk, and tuned from the log.

| Constant | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `RTF` | swift 0.08, apex 0.2, prime 0.45 | Decode seconds per audio second. **Guesses until T2.4 measures them on the M1.** |
| `LAST_CHUNK_BUDGET_S` | 0.7 | Longest the last chunk may take with Apex |
| `PRIME_MIN_TALK_S` | 15 | Load Prime only for talks this long |
| `PRIME_IDLE_S` | 300 | Unload Prime after this idle time |
| `RAM_FLOOR_MB` | 1024 | Never pick Prime under this free RAM |
| `FALLBACK_LEFT_S`, `MIN_HISTORY_ROWS` | 3.0, 30 | Slack guess when his history is too short |

Consequence: every chunk's model, reason, slack and RTF seen go to the log json. `report.py` compares `lean` and `cascade`. The decision is kept or reversed in Phase 6.
Open point (measured 27 Oct 2026): `report.py --left` on 633 dictations gives about 22 s of expected talk time left at every e from 2 to 40 s. The history is long and flat, so slack is large and the rule will mostly pick Prime while he talks. If the A/B shows this hurts, the history is the first thing to tune (for example, recent dictations only).
