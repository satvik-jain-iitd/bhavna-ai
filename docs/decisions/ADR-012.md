# ADR-012: Lean profile on the Mac, and a dynamic encoder window for Apex

Status: accepted, 2026-10-07

## Context
Measured on the owner's M1 8 GB (H3, H3b, experiments.md): Apex through mlx-whisper takes about
5 s for every clip, whether the clip is 1 s or 12 s. The cost is the encoder running over a 30 s
padded window. Cutting the window to 15 s gives 2.8 s, to 10 s gives 2.0 s, with identical text on
the clips tested. fp16 instead of q8 gives nothing. whisper.cpp Metal at 15 s gives 1.9 s.
Apex also transcribes English word-perfectly (H2), so Parakeet buys speed, not accuracy.
The owner's laptop is his daily machine; three resident models (about 2.3 GB) plus Chrome pushed it
into heavy swap, and he asked for RAM consideration.

## Decision
1. `PROFILE = "lean"` is the Mac default: Apex only, about 0.9 GB resident. English goes through
   Apex too. No language-ID model, no Parakeet. `PROFILE = "full"` (tiny + Parakeet + Apex) stays
   one constant away for machines with room.
2. Apex runs with a dynamic encoder window: the clip is padded to the next multiple of 5 s (minimum
   5 s, maximum 30 s) and the encoder's positional table is sliced to that length. Same trick as
   whisper.cpp's `audio_ctx`, done as a five-line patch on the MLX encoder. No new dependency.
3. The window step and the minimum are constants (`WINDOW_STEP_S = 5`). The 5 s window is still
   to be measured for quality (H3b, pending the owner's go-ahead for the run).

## Trade-offs
- Lean gives up Parakeet's 0.45 s English path for a one-model footprint. Expected English latency
  on a 5 s window is about 1 s; measured later.
- A shorter window than the model was trained on can change output on noisy clips. One of 12 clips
  changed at 15 s in whisper.cpp. The quality count per window is recorded before the constant is
  locked.
- whisper.cpp Metal is slightly faster than MLX at the same window. MLX stays for one toolchain;
  revisit if the 5 s MLX window misses 1.2 s.

## Consequences
- Resident RAM on the Mac drops from about 2.3 GB to about 0.9 GB by default.
- ADR-002 (router) still holds for the full profile; lean has nothing to route.
- Benchmarks load one model per process from now on (process.md, RAM rules).
