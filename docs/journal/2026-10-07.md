## Afternoon: D1 benchmarks, first pass

- All models converted and in `models/`: Apex MLX q8 (837 MB), Apex CT2 int8 (792 MB), tiny MLX and CT2, Parakeet v2 MLX (shrunk to bf16, 1.24 GB) and ONNX (3.0 GB, both fp32 and int8), GGML files for the Windows gate (965 MB).
- Two bugs on the way: the conversion env needed `numba`; the new convert.py writes `model.safetensors` but mlx-whisper 0.4.3 loads `weights.safetensors`. Both fixed in `tools/convert.sh`.
- H1 passes: tiny LID gives English 0.96 to 1.00, Hindi and Hinglish 0.00 to 0.43. Auto mode ships.
- Apex output on real human Hindi clips is clean Roman Hinglish ("Kya iska svaad achchha hai?"). Parakeet on the same audio is nonsense, which proves the router earns its 50 ms.
- Latency numbers from the first run are not trustworthy: the Mac had 5.6 GB of 6 GB swap in use (Chrome, FluidVoice and several Claude sessions open). Apex showed 4 to 6 s, Parakeet 0.2 to 0.9 s on short clips but 10 to 25 s on others, which is swap, not compute. Re-running each model alone, then all three with a memory-peak reading.
- Honest note for the owner: a clean latency number needs the other apps closed. The bench row records the swap usage next to the numbers from now on.
